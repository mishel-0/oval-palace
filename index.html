Hospitality & Resorts Project

A Nalakath Holdings Venture

Oval Palace Resort LLP is a landmark hospitality venture in Perinthalmanna, envisioned to deliver a world-class resort experience that blends luxury, comfort, and sustainability. Developed by Nalakath Holdings, the project is strategically located near the Perinthalmanna Cricket Stadium, offering guests a unique destination that combines modern amenities with the natural beauty of Kerala.

Vision: To establish a premier resort that stands as a symbol of hospitality excellence in Malappuram district—catering to leisure travelers, families, corporates, and international tourists alike.
