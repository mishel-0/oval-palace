Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about investing in Oval Palace Resort, the premier hospitality project by Nalakath Holdings in Perinthalmanna, Kerala.

What is Oval Palace Resort?

Oval Palace Resort is a luxury hospitality development by Nalakath Holdings LLP in Perinthalmanna, Kerala. It's an invitation-only investment opportunity where partners hold units in a premium resort with high-ROI potential, long-term capital appreciation, and strategic exit options.

How can I invest in Oval Palace Resort?

Investment is through our partnership program. Submit a request invitation on this page or call +91-799-497-1100. Our investment team will guide you through unit options, ROI projections, and the partnership agreement process.

Where exactly is Oval Palace located?

The resort is in Perinthalmanna, Malappuram district, Kerala, India — near the Perinthalmanna Cricket Stadium. Our office is at ASSN Tower, Opp Suzuki Nexa, Calicut Road, Angadipuram, Perinthalmanna.

What ROI can investors expect?

Oval Palace Resort targets high-yield asset growth through hospitality revenue and property appreciation in Kerala's booming tourism market. Specific ROI projections are shared during private consultations with qualified investors.

Who is Nalakath Holdings?

Nalakath Holdings LLP is a trusted real estate and hospitality developer in Kerala with a proven track record of landmark projects. Based in Perinthalmanna, they specialize in premium asset development and strategic hospitality investments.

When will the resort be operational?

Construction is underway across multiple phases. Visit our Construction Phase page for the latest progress updates, phase-wise completion timelines, and architectural evolution galleries.

Can NRIs invest from Dubai, UAE, or Gulf?

Yes — Oval Palace Resort is structured for NRI investors. Malayalis in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain & Saudi Arabia can invest remotely with full documentation support. No need to travel to Kerala.

What's the minimum NRI investment?

Plans start from 2 units at competitive INR rates. NRI investors get the same pricing as domestic investors plus repatriation benefits. Contact our NRI desk at +91-799-497-1100 for a personalized consultation.
