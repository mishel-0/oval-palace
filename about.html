Chairman – Nalakath Holdings

Mohamed Ashraf Nalakath

With over three decades of diverse experience spanning oil & gas, construction, design, and hospitality, Mohamed Ashraf Nalakath leads Nalakath Holdings with a vision rooted in excellence, innovation, and integrity.

A graduate with a strong foundation in engineering and project management, Mr. Ashraf spent a decade with WS Atkins UK, overseeing Dubai’s most iconic developments, including the Burj Al Arab, Wild Wadi Waterpark, Jumeirah Beach Hotel, and Emirates Towers.

Today, he guides the company’s diversified portfolio with a steadfast commitment to delivering premium quality and sustainable growth, combining strategic foresight with operational excellence.

Executive Director – Nalakath Holdings

Shafeeq Aslam

Shafeeq Aslam is an accomplished healthcare and renewable energy professional with over 20 years of experience working in leading hospitals across India, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE.

In 2015, he established a successful solar energy company in India, delivering numerous high-impact government and private sector projects across Kerala. His leadership reflects a deep commitment to innovation and sustainability.

As Executive Director, Shafeeq combines his extensive international experience and entrepreneurial vision to guide the group’s growth and diversification.
