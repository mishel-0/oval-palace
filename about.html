About the Institution

Redefining Hospitality

Oval Palace is Kerala’s premier luxury resort venture by Nalakath Holdings. It is a strategic fusion of world-class architectural design and institutional-grade investment stability.

Located on the banks of the serene Kadalundi River, this landmark project is engineered to provide an unparalleled sanctuary for guests and a secure, high-yield asset class for our distinguished partners.

Strategic Location

Strategically positioned in the heart of Perinthalmanna, near the Perinthalmanna Cricket Stadium. Our project site offers a unique blend of urban connectivity and serene natural landscapes in the Malappuram district of Kerala.
