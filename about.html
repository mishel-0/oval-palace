About the Institution

Redefining Hospitality

Oval Palace Resort LLP is a landmark hospitality venture in the heart of Perinthalmanna—the region's burgeoning "Hospital City." Envisioned to deliver a world-class experience with panoramic mountain views across Malappuram, this project blends uncompromising luxury with strategic urban growth.

Vision: To establish a premier landmark in a city poised for massive development over the next few decades, catering to leisure, families, and international tourists while setting a symbol of hospitality excellence in Kerala.
