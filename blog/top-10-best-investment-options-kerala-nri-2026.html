Top 10 Best Investment Options in Kerala for NRIs in 2026

July 2, 2026 · 12 min read · By Nalakath Holdings

For Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) from Kerala, especially Gulf Malayalis working across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain — finding the best investment in Kerala is a priority. Your hard-earned money needs to work hard while you're away, and Kerala offers some compelling options.

In this guide, we rank the top 10 best investment options in Kerala for NRI investors in 2026, evaluating each on ROI, safety, liquidity, and suitability for Gulf Malayalis.

1

Hospitality Investment — Resort Units in Kerala

Best for: High ROI, passive income, capital appreciation

ROI: Strong — targeting 12-18% annual returns through hospitality revenue + property appreciation

Recommended investment: Oval Palace Resort in Perinthalmanna

Hospitality investment — buying premium resort units — is emerging as the best investment in Kerala for NRI investors in 2026. Unlike traditional real estate, resort units generate active hospitality income while the property itself appreciates. Projects like Oval Palace Resort offer NRI-friendly documentation, flexible exits, and complete management — making it truly passive.

Why it wins: Dual income stream (revenue + appreciation), professional management, lower entry barrier than land/building, and NRI repatriation support.

2

Kerala Real Estate — Land & Plots

ROI: 8-12% annual appreciation in growing corridors

Land in Kerala has appreciated steadily, especially in developing corridors like Malappuram, Thrissur, and Kochi outskirts. However, it requires active management, legal due diligence, and carries risks of encroachment or title disputes for absentee owners.

3

NRE Fixed Deposits

ROI: 6-8% per annum

Safe, liquid, and entirely hassle-free. NRE FDs offer guaranteed returns and full repatriability. The downside? Returns barely keep pace with inflation, and they lack the wealth-building potential of hospitality or real estate investment.

4

Mutual Funds & Equity (NRE/NRO)

ROI: 10-15% (market-linked)

NRIs can invest in Indian mutual funds through NRE/NRO accounts. Offers diversification and liquidity. However, market volatility and currency fluctuation risk are real concerns.

5

National Pension System (NPS)

ROI: 9-11% (blended equity/debt)

Tax-efficient retirement savings with NRI eligibility. Locked-in till 60, which is a drawback for younger investors seeking shorter-term goals.

6

Kerala Tourism & Resort Stocks

ROI: Variable, 12-20% in good years

Invest in publicly listed hospitality and tourism companies with exposure to Kerala. Indirect play on the sector without direct property ownership.

7

Gold & Sovereign Gold Bonds

ROI: 7-10% (including 2.5% interest)

Traditional favorite for Malayalis. SGBs offer better safety than physical gold with annual interest. Low liquidity compared to other options.

8

Rental Residential Properties

ROI: 3-5% rental yield + 6-8% appreciation

Buying apartments or houses for rental income in Kerala cities. Tenant management from abroad is challenging, and rental yields in Kerala are historically lower than commercial properties.

9

Commercial Real Estate

ROI: 6-10% rental yield + appreciation

Shop spaces, office buildings in urban Kerala. Higher entry point, requires professional tenant management.

10

Government Small Savings Schemes (PPF, NSC)

ROI: 7-8%

Safe but low returns. NRI eligibility is restricted for some schemes. Minimal management but also minimal growth.

Why Hospitality Investment Leads for NRIs in Kerala

Here's why hospitality investment — specifically premium resort units — ranks as the best investment in Kerala for NRI investors:

  • Dual Returns: You earn from hospitality operations (room revenue, F&B, events) AND property appreciation — two income streams from one investment
  • Professional Management: The developer manages everything — bookings, maintenance, staffing, compliance. You don't deal with tenants or property managers
  • NRI Documentation: Projects like Oval Palace Resort offer complete NRI support, including repatriation guidance, NRE/NRO account coordination, and remote investment
  • Lower Entry than Land: Resort units are priced competitively compared to buying land and building
  • Flexible Exit: Unlike land that takes months to sell, resort unit investment structures often include buyback or resale options

What Gulf Malayalis Need to Know

For NRIs based in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Kuwait City, or Riyadh — investing in Kerala has become significantly easier than a decade ago:

  • NRE/NRO Accounts: You can invest directly from your NRE account with full repatriability
  • Remote Investment: Legal documentation can be completed via power of attorney — no need to travel to Kerala
  • INR/AED Transparency: Reputable developers provide clear pricing in both currencies
  • RERA Registration: Most Kerala real estate projects are RERA-registered, ensuring legal protection
