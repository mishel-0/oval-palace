Why Hospitality Investment in Kerala is the Smartest NRI Choice

July 2, 2026 · 10 min read · By Nalakath Holdings

For decades, NRIs from Kerala have defaulted to two investments: fixed deposits and agricultural land. Both have their place, but neither delivers the kind of returns that build generational wealth.

There's a new leader emerging, and it's hospitality investment in Kerala. Premium resort units are outperforming traditional asset classes on ROI, passive income, and long-term appreciation. Here's why smart Gulf Malayalis are making the switch.

The Problem with Traditional NRI Investments

Before we explore why hospitality investment wins, let's look at what NRIs have been putting their money into and why these options are falling short:

❌ NRE Fixed Deposits — Safe but Stagnant

At 6-8% annual returns, NRE FDs barely beat inflation. A €1,00,000 deposit grows to €1,48,000 in 5 years — while real inflation eats half that growth. You're losing purchasing power, not building wealth.

❌ Land & Agricultural Property — Illiquid & High Maintenance

Land in Kerala appreciates but selling takes 6-12 months. Encroachment, title disputes, and absentee management are constant headaches for Gulf-based NRIs. Plus, land gives you zero income while you hold it.

❌ Residential Rental Properties — Low Yield, High Hassle

Kerala residential properties deliver just 3-5% rental yield. Managing tenants from Dubai or Doha is exhausting — late payments, repairs, vacancies all add stress. The ROI doesn't justify the effort.

Why Hospitality Investment in Kerala is Different

Hospitality investment — buying a unit in a professionally managed resort or hotel property — solves every problem above. Here's how:

✅ Dual Income Stream

Your resort unit generates hospitality revenue (room bookings, F&B, events, weddings) AND appreciates in value. Unlike land that sits idle, your resort unit works 365 days a year, earning from Kerala's booming tourism industry.

✅ Zero Management Burden

The developer's professional management team handles everything — reservations, housekeeping, maintenance, guest services, legal compliance. You receive your share of revenue without lifting a finger. This is true passive income.

✅ Built-in Exit Strategy

Premium hospitality projects like Oval Palace Resort offer structured exit options, including unit resale through the developer's network. You're not trapped in an illiquid asset.

✅ NRI-Specific Documentation

Hospitality investment in Kerala is designed for NRI investors. Complete repatriation support, NRE/NRO account coordination, remote investment via power of attorney — everything works from abroad.

Kerala's Tourism Boom: The Tailwind Behind Hospitality Investment

Hospitality investment in Kerala is riding a powerful wave. Kerala's tourism sector has been growing at 10-15% annually, with domestic tourism surging post-pandemic. Key trends driving demand:

  • Wedding tourism: Kerala is India's top wedding destination, with luxury resorts commanding premium rates during wedding season (October-March)
  • Corporate retreats: Companies are investing in offsite venues, and premium resorts in Malappuram-Perinthalmanna corridor are gaining corporate bookings
  • Gulf Malayali diaspora travel: NRIs returning for vacations prefer luxury resort experiences over traditional homestays
  • Domestic luxury travel: Growing Indian middle class is driving demand for premium resort experiences in Kerala

This tourism growth directly translates to higher occupancy rates and revenue for Kerala hospitality investment owners.
